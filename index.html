IPresentation

SEL by ÓX is a modern restaurant built on seasonal ingredients, simplicity and quality craftsmanship. Operated by the team behind the Michelin-starred restaurant ÓX, it offers a five-course tasting menu that changes every three weeks, in harmony with the seasons and the finest Icelandic produce. The cooking rests on Icelandic ingredients, French culinary technique and subtle Asian influences, served in a warm and relaxed room.

IIIThe room

A warm room under antique lights from Holland.

Book a table

On Laugavegur 55: a long oak counter facing the kitchen, tables under the tapestry walls and a wall of glass-fronted wine cabinets. The relaxed little sister of ÓX, open Wednesday to Saturday.
